Six years in brief

From permits and COVID to opening, buildout, and this summer's weatherization.
  • Jan 2020~$1.5M raised; permits finally cleared after months with Planning & Health.
  • Mar 2020COVID closes all three of the operator's venues; project pauses, rent abatements negotiated.
  • Dec 2020Ground broken. GC dropped in favor of in-house construction management to cut ~20% of cost.
  • 2021Post-tension-slab pergola engineering, SFMTA crane-permit delays, supply chain. Raise reaches $2.23M plus a Republic.co crowdfunding campaign.
  • Feb 2022Soft opening Feb 9–11; grand opening Feb 16. First year closes at $4.88M revenue, +$473k.
  • 2023$5.27M revenue; weatherization (heaters, windshields) begins paying off; strong SF press coverage.
  • 2024−$460k loss on cold-season softness; profits redirected earlier into the ground-floor buildout.
  • 2025Recovery to $5.15M and roughly break-even.
  • 2026H1 revenue +9.9% YoY, back to positive net income. ~$400k rooftop weatherization & wind-protection build underway (operator-funded loan; completion ~early Sept). Q3 dips while a third of seating is offline; operator expects the strongest Q4 since opening.
